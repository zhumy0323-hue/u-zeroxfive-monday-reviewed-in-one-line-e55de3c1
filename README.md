## What this entry is

A one-line review of my Monday, written as a product review of a day that
delivered nothing and still sent an invoice: tired at wake-up, tired at work,
tired at bedtime — and somehow I still owe Tuesday an apology. The entry
itself is just that one line: no title, no explanation, nothing else.

## Why it's funny

It treats Monday like a bad purchase — zero stars, would not relive — then
lands the twist everyone recognises: for all the complaining, I'll be back
next week anyway. The joke is the loop: exhaustion all day, an apology owed
to a day that hasn't happened yet, and a refund policy of none.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the single-line entry to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, one line,
no trailing newline, under 200 characters). The program asserts the length
and format before and after writing, and running it twice produces
byte-identical output.
